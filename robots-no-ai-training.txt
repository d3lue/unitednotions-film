#                       It does not change the place of the site in Google Search.
#   Applebot-Extended   Apple: training of its generative models. Applebot itself still reads the site for search.
#   CCBot               Common Crawl: an open archive of the web that AI developers use
#   meta-externalagent  Meta: training of its models
#   Amazonbot           Amazon: pages may be used to train its models
#
# A crawler obeys this file only when its maker decides so. These seven say they do.
