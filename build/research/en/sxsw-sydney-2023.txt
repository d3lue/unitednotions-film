T: La Lucha and Prison X at SXSW Sydney
D: Experience cutting-edge cinema and XR animation at SXSW Sydney’s film events. Don’t miss the Australian premiere of “La Lucha,” a gripping social justice documentary by Violeta Ayala. It showcases an extraordinary protest by Bolivians with disabilities, capturing their brave journey through the Andes for pension rights. Next, immerse yourself in the surreal world of “Prison X,” a ground-breaking XR animation experience set in a Neo-Andean underworld. Navigate a labyrinth filled with devils, saints, and power dynamics. Both films are directed by acclaimed filmmaker Violeta Ayala and presented by UnitedNotions.Film.
