T: La Lucha receives support for outreach
D: Explore ‘La Lucha,’ a documentary capturing the incredible journey of people with disabilities fighting for a pension in Bolivia. This page shares the news of generous support from the Perspective Fund and offers an invitation to join the outreach across Latin America. Directed by Violeta Ayala and featuring the courageous efforts of Feliza Ali Ramos and Rose Mery Guarita Uribe, ‘La Lucha’ is more than a film; it’s a legacy of courage and change. Stay connected with updates and opportunities to share this tribute to those who dare to defy the status quo.

P: When a group of people with disabilities united in protest for a pension in Bolivia, they embarked on an unforgettable journey, traversing the Andean mountains in their wheelchairs. ‘La Lucha’ stands as a tribute to their courage and defiance, a story that we’ve been honored to capture in our documentary. Following our premiere at [BlackStar](note:aug-19-2023) ⭐️ the response has been ♥️ 💜 🌼🖤🩶🌹💙🌵🩵🩷🌷❤️.
P: Now, we’re excited to bring ‘La Lucha’ to communities across Latin America, sharing this powerful tale of resistance. The outreach initiative, generously backed by the [Perspective Fund](https://www.perspectivefund.org), is close to our hearts. We, [Violeta Ayala](https://en.m.wikipedia.org/wiki/Violeta_Ayala), [Feliza Ali Ramos](https://www.jica.go.jp/Resource/english/news/field/2022/20230105_11.html), and [Rose Mery Guarita Uribe](https://www.huffpost.com/entry/operacion-ginebra_b_11676622), invite you to connect with us if you’re inspired to share this story with your community. With Life Media Projects as our Fiscal Sponsor, we’re reaching out, building connections, and continuing the fight.
P: Follow us on [Instagram](https://instagram.com/lalucha.red) and our [website](https://lalucha.red) to stay updated, and join us in sharing ‘La Lucha,’ a tribute to all who dare to defy the status quo.
