T: From documentary to living systems
D: Across documentary filmmaking, computational creativity, XR, affective computing, and real-time creature animation, United Notions Film’s research archive traces the evolution of a new cinematic language emerging from Cochabamba. Combining protests, fiber optics, Blender pipelines, AI systems, sensors, somatic puppeteering, and collective memory, the work explores how bodies, infrastructures, environments, and machines interact as interconnected living systems. The archive functions simultaneously as political testimony, technical documentation, laboratory notebook, and speculative cinema in motion.
