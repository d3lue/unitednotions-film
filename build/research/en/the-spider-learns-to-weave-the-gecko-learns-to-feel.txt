T: The spider learns to weave, the gecko learns to feel, and SIGGRAPH calls
D: In Week 9 of the koa.xyz computational creativity lab, Violeta Ayala, Dan Fallshaw, and Brian Condori developed new real-time creature behaviors across the spider and gecko pipelines while preparing the SIGGRAPH 2026 submission for Huk’s World: Somatic Puppeteering. The spider evolved from a static FK rig into a walking, finger-controlled web-weaving creature with autonomous orb-web construction, while the gecko’s emotional system was rebuilt after discovering stale sensor data trapped her permanently in fear responses. The week explored movement, recovery, memory, and how computational creatures inherit emotion from both code and human bodies.

P: There were other ghosts. hand_near was saying a hand was close even when there was no hand. The red mood color was described as “blood and fire, danger,” which made Qwen overreact. The voice threshold was too high, so normal speech went unheard. is_talking was being sent as 0/1 but read as a boolean, which is the kind of tiny misunderstanding that can change a whole personality.
P: None of these bugs were huge. Together, they made a creature who could not relax.
