T: 15 creatures, 14 pipelines and a platform-agnostic brain
D: After two months of intensive development, the koa.xyz computational creativity lab established a platform-agnostic real-time puppeteering framework where Python-driven creature brains operate across Blender and VR worlds in Resonite. Using hand tracking, heartbeat sensors, environmental data, local AI models, and neural networks, the system allows plants, whales, geckos, spiders, orchids, and other creatures to respond live to bodies, emotions, fire, water, and movement without motion-capture suits or traditional animation pipelines. Built from Cochabamba and grounded in Indigenous knowledge systems, the project now spans 15 creatures and 14 interconnected pipelines exploring cinema, AI, somatic interaction, and computational worldbuilding.
