WHAT IS IN THIS FOLDER

  site/                        the site. Everything inside it goes on the server.
  build/                       the workshop that writes the pages. It stays on a computer.
  htaccess.txt                 a spare copy of site/.htaccess, in case the hidden file gets lost
  robots-no-ai-training.txt    the other robots.txt (see ROBOTS at the end)

  To look at the site on your own computer, paste this in Terminal from inside this folder:
    cd site && (sleep 1 && open http://localhost:8765 &) && python3 -m http.server 8765
  The Spanish site is at http://localhost:8765/es/
  That small server knows nothing of the rules in .htaccess, so there the short addresses (/about) do not answer.
  The links of the pages all work.

PUT IT ON DREAMHOST

  1. In the DreamHost panel, add unitednotions.film as a website with hosting.
     It must be the usual Apache hosting. The site depends on its file .htaccess, and DreamHost says
     an .htaccess file does not work on DreamPress, which runs Nginx.
  2. The folder of the site on the server is /home/USERNAME/unitednotions.film
     Make an SFTP user for this site alone. Keep its password out of this folder and out of any chat.
  3. Upload by SFTP everything that is inside site/ into that folder. The folder site itself does not go up.
     Two files start with a dot, and a Mac hides them:
         site/.htaccess        the rules of the server
         site/es/.htaccess     one line, for the Spanish pages
     In Finder, Command Shift . shows them. In the SFTP program, turn on "show hidden files".
     Check on the server that both arrived. Files should be 644 and folders 755.
     build/ and the two spare files stay at home.
  4. Look at the site before the domain moves. DreamHost describes two ways:
     a hosts file on your computer, or a free dreamhosters.com subdomain. The pages link to each other
     with relative addresses, so both ways show the whole site.
  5. Try these on the server:
         /                                       the maze, in English
         /es/                                    the maze, in Spanish
         /about      /research      /people      the short addresses
         /research/the-whale-that-watches-back   a lab note, at the address it had on the old site
         /for-press-media                        an address of the old site: it must land on /press
         /this-is-wrong                          the 404 page, in the look of the site
         /es/esto-no-existe                      the 404 page in Spanish
         /robots.txt   /llms.txt   /es/llms.txt   /sitemap.xml
  6. Move the domain. At the company where unitednotions.film is registered, set the nameservers to
         ns1.dreamhost.com   ns2.dreamhost.com   ns3.dreamhost.com
     DreamHost says the change takes 4 to 72 hours.
     Before that, copy to DreamHost every DNS record the domain uses today. Email comes first:
     if the domain receives mail, its MX records must exist at DreamHost before the move.
  7. HTTPS. DreamHost says it sends visitors to https by itself once the certificate is installed.
     Nothing in .htaccess forces it, so the site also works in the hours before the certificate.
  8. www. Every page names itself https://unitednotions.film, without www.
     In the panel, choose to remove www (DreamHost: "Add or remove www from a domain").
  9. When the site answers at its own name, give Google the map: in Search Console, add
         https://unitednotions.film/sitemap.xml

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG

  "500 Internal Server Error" right after the upload
      One line of .htaccess is not allowed on that server. Put a # in front of the lines of one block
      at a time until the site comes back. Start with the block OPTIONS. Then tell Violeta which block it was.
  The home page works, /about.html works, /about says 404
      .htaccess did not arrive. Upload htaccess.txt into the folder of the site and rename it .htaccess
  Spanish letters look broken in /es/llms.txt
      Same cause: .htaccess did not arrive.
  A page shows without its pictures
      The folder assets did not finish uploading. It holds about 100 MB.

WHAT THE RULES IN .htaccess DO

  Short addresses. /about shows about.html, /research/a-note shows research/a-note.html.
      These are the addresses the pages give to Google, and the ones the old site used.
  The old site. Its 50 addresses lead to the new pages. /for-press-media, /films, /xr, /now-playing,
      /research-archive and the seven /news/... pages are sent on. /birdbot and /educational-collection
      are not built yet and lead home for now.
  A wrong address gets the 404 page of the site, in English or in Spanish.
  A folder shows no list of its files.
  build/, README.txt and this file answer 404 if someone uploads them by mistake.
  Browsers keep the pictures 30 days, the stylesheet and the script a year, a page ten minutes.
  Text travels compressed.

  Tested on Apache 2.4 on another machine: 150 checks, all passed, in three set-ups of the server.
  In a fourth, without the modules for keeping and compressing, every address still answered.
  The checks cover every page in both languages, every address of the old site, every address in sitemap.xml
  and every link in the two llms.txt. The rules have not run on DreamHost itself yet. Step 5 is that test.

TO CHANGE THE SITE LATER

  The texts are in build/content.py, build/content_pages.py, build/strings_es.py and build/research/.
  Then:   python3 build/build.py      It rewrites site/. About two minutes.
  It needs Python 3 with:   pip3 install pillow numpy fonttools brotli
  Upload site/ again. The same texts and pictures always give the same pages.
  robots.txt, sitemap.xml, llms.txt, es/llms.txt, the 404 pages and .htaccess are written by the build too.
  Their words are in build/extras.py.

ROBOTS

  site/robots.txt is open to everyone: people, search engines, archives and AI systems.
  The old site was open in the same way.
  robots-no-ai-training.txt is the other choice. It keeps search engines and AI assistants that link to
  their source, and asks seven crawlers that collect pages to train AI models to stay out.
  To use it, rename it robots.txt and put it in site/ in place of the other one.
  Violeta decides which one goes live.

  site/llms.txt is a guide to the site for language models, in English. site/es/llms.txt is the same in Spanish.
