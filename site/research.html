Research

The lab is in Cochabamba. It builds creatures, sensors and worlds that respond to bodies, environments, memory and emotion in real time. These notes are its archive: experiments, failures and breakthroughs.

2026 152025 62023 9

2026

  • 2 June 2026, Cochabamba

    The jaguaress learns her own skeleton

    Huk the Jaguaress recovered her twenty-four real poses from an export that only looked finished: a faithful carousel exposed a smooth interpolation, an exporter rewritten to read the rig’s true keyframes brought the body back, and the question turned from how she moves to why.

  • 26 May 2026, Cochabamba

    The whale that watches back

    YakuMama reads the colour of the room and answers with mood and song, decaying and reweighting ten emotions every frame, diving when the water is taken and singing when the rain comes, all running locally on one machine with no cloud.

  • 22 May 2026, Cochabamba

    The body decides the interface

    The gecko learns finger puppeteering, the monkey begins to mirror directly, Huk the Jaguaress discovers her own skeletal logic, and a taxonomy of locomotion emerges.

  • 19 May 2026, Cochabamba

    The monkey learns the shape of its own body

    Procedural tails, swing-twist quaternion decomposition, performer-centered quadruped animation, and the discovery that rest poses themselves carry assumptions about bodies, movement, and intelligence.

  • 9 May 2026, Cochabamba

    The monkey learns to move: quaternions, humanoid puppeteering

    Quaternion retargeting, hybrid body-hand tracking, spine cascade systems, and the first full-body humanoid creature pipeline inside our ecosystem.

  • 6 May 2026, Cochabamba

    The llama learns to feel, the model learns to listen, and Yakumama is born

    Neural network mood training, cardiac breathing systems, Yakumama’s emergence, and ch’ixi philosophy entering the animation pipeline.

  • 28 April 2026, Cochabamba

    When the heartbeat moves the flower

    Pulse sensors, ESP32 systems, systole and diastole translated into real-time plant animation and embodied computational creativity.

  • 25 April 2026, Cochabamba

    The spider learns to weave, the gecko learns to feel, and SIGGRAPH calls

    Orb-web systems, gecko emotional memory, Qwen integration, and SIGGRAPH documentation.

  • 18 April 2026, Cochabamba

    The gecko learns to feel, Qwen 3.6 finds its voice, and the recording begins

    Sensor fusion, emotional state systems, flavor memories, AI narration, and real-time creature cognition.

  • 17 April 2026, Cochabamba

    15 creatures, 14 pipelines and a platform-agnostic brain

    Building real-time creature intelligence systems across Blender, VR, sensors, and XR worlds.

  • 10 April 2026, Cochabamba

    The water lily learns to breathe, the sundew counts fingers, and the pipeline moves to WebSocket

    Victoria Regia water sensing, Drosera finger tracking, hummingbird gaze systems, and WebSocket architecture migration.

  • 30 March 2026, Cochabamba

    The tree learns fear, the orchid mourns fire, and the llama finds stillness

    NASA wildfire feeds, shy trees with infrared sensing, and calibration systems for nonhuman creature embodiment.

  • 21 March 2026, Cochabamba

    The $2 gyroscope that solved what the neural network couldn’t

    Sensor fusion, 360° body tracking, gyroscopes, MediaPipe limitations, and embodied spatial intelligence.

  • 14 March 2026, Cochabamba

    Toward embodied intelligence for nonhuman characters

    Depth sensing, robotic cameras, MediaPipe integration, and spatial movement systems beyond human pose datasets.

  • 8 March 2026, Cochabamba

    Somatic puppeteering across plants, birds and heartbeats

    March 2026 research sprint exploring real-time body-driven creature systems across plants, condors, shaders, and pulse-responsive environments.

2025

  • 29 November 2025, Cochabamba

    When documentary begins to think

    Two weeks on two fronts. In the lab, motion systems and pixel surfaces that react to movement. In the streets of Cochabamba, the Comteco vigil filmed and streamed as it happens.

  • 17 November 2025, Cochabamba

    Technologies of distribution, Comteco and the fight for transparency

    For 88 days the senior shareholders of the telecom cooperative Comteco hold a vigil for elections, audits and answers. TikTok becomes a living archive of the protest.

  • 15 November 2025, Cochabamba

    From documentary to living systems

    Affective computing between Montreal and La Paz, new worlds for Huk, and public subsidies, music interfaces, electoral data and aqueducts read as technologies of distribution.

  • 26 June 2025, Guangzhou

    The condor obeyed Violeta’s hand

    At the opening of Surreality in Guangzhou a digital condor follows Violeta Ayala’s hand in real time. No motion capture suit. An early public demonstration of somatic puppeteering.

  • April 2025, Copenhagen

    Building Huk V1.0: a digital deity for the burning Amazon

    Huk the Jaguaress begins with the fires in the Amazon. Her first version connects wildfire alerts, plant bioelectricity, dancers and edge AI.

  • 12 February 2025, Nottingham

    Two machines, one question: feminist AI at the VIP Studio

    Violeta Ayala and Yasmeen Hitti join the Feminist AI residency at the University of Nottingham’s VIP Studio. They build the VIP Chatbot, where OpenAI and DeepSeek answer the same prompt side by side.
