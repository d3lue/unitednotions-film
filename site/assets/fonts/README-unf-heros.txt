UNF Heros and UNF Heros Condensed

These three files are subsets of TeX Gyre Heros, made by the GUST e-foundry:

  unf-heros-condensed-bold.woff2   from texgyreheroscn-bold.otf
  unf-heros-regular.woff2          from texgyreheros-regular.otf
  unf-heros-bold.woff2             from texgyreheros-bold.otf

Changes: glyphs outside Latin text were removed, the files were converted
to WOFF2 and the fonts were renamed, as the licence requests for derived works.

The original fonts: https://www.gust.org.pl/projects/e-foundry/tex-gyre/heros
Licence: GUST Font License, see GUST-FONT-LICENSE.txt in this folder.

The site asks the visitor's computer for Helvetica Neue first. These files
are what everyone else sees.
