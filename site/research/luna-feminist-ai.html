Violeta Ayala’s mission: creating LUNA, the future of feminist AI

Violeta Ayala introduces LUNA, Learning Unbiased Neural Algorithm: a project and a documentary about building an AI that challenges bias.

  • September 2023
  • Sydney
  • Lab note

In a world where technology influences society, Violeta Ayala, a filmmaker, embarks on a revolutionary journey. She introduces LUNA (Learning Unbiased Neural Algorithm), a project aiming to challenge the biases in artificial intelligence and offer hope for an unbiased AI.

The story of LUNA starts with a group of women with diverse perspectives coming together to create an AI that embodies equity. It's not only about technology; it's about changing the narrative and envisioning an AI that supports feminist values.

Ayala faces obstacles along the way. Her documentary reveals the difficulties of collaboration, fighting against tech giants, and infusing LUNA with principles of equity. It's a personal and collective journey to address biases in current AI systems.

Through Ayala's documentary, viewers get an inside look at the creation of LUNA. This story parallels the impact of historical inventions like the printing press, but with a focus on addressing bias in AI. It poses questions about dismantling biases in technology and shifting power dynamics for true transformation.

With support from Chicken and Egg through a Netflix grant, this documentary sparks a movement for creating AI that promotes social equity. It challenges us to imagine a future where technology is a force for equity, redefining the narrative of artificial intelligence through a feminist perspective.

As the story of Feminist AI unfolds, it invites us to join a movement shaping the future of technology. It's a call to action for technologists, feminists, and anyone dreaming of a future where technology benefits everyone equally.

This isn't just about an documentary or an AI named LUNA; it's about the potential for collective action and visionary thinking to reshape the world.
