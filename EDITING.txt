United Notions Film: how to change the site
Written 7 October 2026, the day the site went live on DreamHost.

THE SHAPE OF IT

  The site is plain pages, written by a small program from text files. There is no database and no login.
  The text files are the site. Change them, run one command, and the pages are rebuilt and uploaded.

  site/        the pages, as the server has them. Never edit these by hand: the next build overwrites them.
  build/       the program and the texts it reads.
      research/en/<name>.txt      a lab note, in English
      research/es/<name>.txt      the same note, in Spanish
      content.py                  the works on the home page
      content_pages.py            About, People, News, Film Futurism, Press
      strings_es.py               the Spanish of everything that is not a lab note
      picture-in/                 pictures of new lab notes go here
      video-in/                   videos of new lab notes go here (also the 28 originals of the old site: 1.2 GB, keep them)
      data/research.json          the list of lab notes with their date and place

THE ONE COMMAND

      sh publish.sh

  It makes web copies of any new picture or video, builds the pages (about two minutes), uploads what changed
  and checks 34 addresses of the live site. From a new Mac, once:   pip3 install --user pillow numpy fonttools brotli
  and   brew install ffmpeg   for the videos.

A NEW LAB NOTE

  1.   python3 build/new_note.py "The title of the note" --place Cochabamba
       It makes the two text files and adds the note to the list. It prints where everything goes.
  2.   Write build/research/en/<name>.txt. One block per line, each line starts with a tag:
           T: title       D: one sentence for search engines       S: one-sentence summary      (these three first, in this order)
           P: paragraph   H: heading   LI: list item   CAP: caption of the picture or video above
           IMG: 1         the first picture               IMG: 2 | https://address   makes it a link
           VIDEO: 01 | 16:9     a video; 16:9, 9:16 or 1:1 is its shape
           YT: id | title       a YouTube video            VM: id | title     a Vimeo video
           A: text | https://address      a link on its own line
       Inside a line, a link is written [text](https://address).
  3.   Pictures: build/picture-in/<key>-01.jpg, <key>-02.png ...  The key is the short name the script printed.
       Videos:   build/video-in/<key>-01.mp4 (or .mov, any usual format).
       A video of a few seconds plays by itself, silently, in a loop. A longer one waits for the visitor.
  4.   Spanish: build/research/es/<name>.txt. Same lines, same tags, same order, translated.
       build/research/BRIEF-es.md has Violeta's rules. Until it is translated the Spanish site shows the English.
  5.   For people who cannot see the pictures: build/research/alts.json, a sentence per picture, in both languages.
  6.   sh publish.sh

CHANGING A NOTE THAT EXISTS

  Edit its text files in build/research/en and es. Then   sh publish.sh
  Its date and place are in build/data/research.json.

THE OTHER PAGES

  About, People, News, Film Futurism, Press: build/content_pages.py. The works of the home page: build/content.py.
  They are Python files, but the texts in them are plain: find the sentence, change it, keep the quotes around it.
  Their Spanish is in build/strings_es.py, keyed by the English sentence.
  Then   sh publish.sh

FROM A BROWSER, WITHOUT A MAC

  The folder is a git repository. When it is on GitHub, every push to the branch main builds and uploads the site
  by itself (.github/workflows/publish.yml, about three minutes). So a note can be written or corrected on github.com,
  in the browser: open build/research/en/<name>.txt, press the pencil, change it, "Commit changes". Done.
  A new note from the browser needs its line in build/data/research.json too (copy the shape of the last one).
  New videos cannot go that way: they are too big for git. Add them from a Mac with sh publish.sh.
  New pictures can: upload them to build/picture-in.

WHEN SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG

  The build prints what is missing: a picture whose web copy is not made, a video it is waiting for,
  a Spanish text that does not exist. Read the last lines.
  sh check.sh   tries the 34 addresses of the live site and names any that fails.
